Quote of the Week

The best moments in our lives, are not the passive, receptive, relaxing times, although such experiences can also be enjoyable, if we have worked hard to attain them. The best moments usually occur when a person’s body or mind is stretched to its limits in a voluntary effort to accomplish something difficult and worthwhile.

— Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi, Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience

A bit long one this week, but a good one. This phase of my work at docsumo might be the best memories I would reflect then. Right now it feels a bit frustrating, not the work, not the process but just rhe shear act of making it work. That is engineering, sometimes we feel everything should work, but thats what our job is to “make it work”. It wont work, that why we are software engineers, to make it work.
