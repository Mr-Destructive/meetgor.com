Week #104

A soul sucking week, draining nonetheless. Worked almost everyday from 9 to 10. More than 10 hours definitely. Not that its not fun, but it gets unsustainable if I just forget what I am doing with my back and body.

I have got 0 reading done for this week, seriously 0. Not even books. I completed one book over the last weekend but that’s about it. Nothing else from that. Feels a bit rough week to be honest. I thought I might not be able to write this newsletter just as the time is 2 years about to be completed. It has been 2 years exactly this Monday will be 2 years since I wrote the #0 edition of this newsletter. Not a week missed. I feel good now.

I don’t know how to define a software engineer now, the part of software is slowly or rapidly shifted from just code to problems. How to orchaestrate and manage these agentic tools. I am not hyping it, people using it effectively are some skilled ones. Maybe its a specific niche but that’s what the world is hungry for now at the moment. So need to learn it rather than wait for it to pass.

Quote of the Week

“Perhaps it takes a broken hear to prevent other to break theirs”

— Thrity Umrigar in Bombay Time

Beautifully said. Someone would have had a heartbreak for be able to sympathize with the others and help prevent their hearts from breaking. Empathy is important to sustain this world of ours.
