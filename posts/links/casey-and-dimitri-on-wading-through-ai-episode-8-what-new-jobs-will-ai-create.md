- Casey and Dimitri on Wading through AI Episode 8: What new jobs will AI create
- Yes what exactly. I like the questions more than the answers. They make me also think on what I like to see in the world.
- The example of 1 vs 5 person company is so real. It divides the job we assume, but it creates a divide in who the actual winner is, you can’t say I see one video on youtube and other video on dailymotion or other service. One just is better than the other, for some use case some software might be good but it can’t be both for all. That was a worth learning perspective, AI is not going to divide or shrink the profits its just going to hammer one and leave the rest empty handed.
- I thought it might create more companies, but the companies might not be big, so profit too would be meager, so its not the same equation anymore. It would be terribly different.
