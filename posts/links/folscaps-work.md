## Commentary

- Folscaps work
- This is interesting product. I does everything for your book except to write it. Brilliant idea. I can think of it as a peer or a assistant giving me all the details and the nuances of the things I want.
- But the counter point here is the friction is what creates those connections in the story. Like if i want to look up something I wrote in other chapter or other scene, while reading some other parts I might discover a flaw or a idea might strike, whereas when its handed over in a instant, it becomes harder and harder to stick to the story.
