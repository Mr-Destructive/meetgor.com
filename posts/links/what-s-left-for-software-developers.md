## Commentary

- What’s left for software developers
- That is a good post. When software generation gets cheaper, all the things around it gets valuable like user-experience, marketing, domain expertise and so on. The product market fit gap is going to climb high as the production of software gets cheaper, the ability to build the “right” thing is the moat now (which always was, but its more than ever now).
