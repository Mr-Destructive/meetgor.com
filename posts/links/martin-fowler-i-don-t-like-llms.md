## Commentary

- Martin Fowler: I don’t like LLMs
- Perfect. They find working and interacting with humans over a period of time satisfying and rewarding as opposed to working with an LLM. Who can argue with this point. I can’t and I won’t because there is no counter point for it.
- LLMs don’t have the care and emotion that a human can show while interacting, it can only glob about how good or bad the current situation or problem at hand is. Its just approximating how a human would write, nothing much.
