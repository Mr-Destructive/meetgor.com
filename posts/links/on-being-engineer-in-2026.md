## Commentary

- Shahar Glazner’s Blog: On being engineer in 2026
- Banger of a post. “our leverage isn’t just in what we know, but in everything we’ve experienced that made us who we are” What a line in the end. Hits home.
- We have been seeing the AI labs saying “We will not need software developers in 6 months”, “Software is mostly solved”, (say that in primeagent’s accent please)
- But that was in 2024, we are in 2026-27 phase, what is happening? Why are software developers still productive when they were meant to be obsolete? I think the easiest answer is no non-technical person wants to take the effort to build software. As simple as it can get, we have the most bizzare urge to learn something that will make something work, and that is what makes a software developers, nerds. Nobody is ready to just prompt it and assume it will work, the quirks, the underlying patterns is something you can’t ask a agent to do, because what will you ask? “Code as a software developer”, well good luck.
