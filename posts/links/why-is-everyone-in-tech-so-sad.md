## Commentary

- Noema Magzine: Why is everyone in tech so sad
- This is a beautiful article. It gives me a sad eye opening view on tech, or people may call it “Knowledge work”. I though that was hard to replace, it still is, but for software engineers, the grunt work of writing and managing the code is gone, so that leaves us with the most exhausting yet satisfying work left to do. Which is of course to make decisions and judgements. Being a manager.
- That was the last thing I was about to do in my life, yet that is what is full of my job now. Weird times. Software engineering rewrote the way we think about software with AI.
